# Direction

## Facts

- Dialog, timing, palette, typography words and bans come from the user's brief (chat, 2026-11-20). No factual claims on screen.
- Korean words: 블로킹 = blocking, 동선 = movement line, 구도 = composition, 카메라 = camera, 끝 = the end — standard Korean.
- Voice: ElevenLabs eleven_v4, voice "Chris Lue - Calm, Warm & Friendly" (dMAZaYG6mMYKX8gr3V9M), one take, cut into 5 phrases placed per 2 s slot.

## Brief

- **Asked for:** Dakwah IMAN motion graphic, 10 s, seated man speaking English, Korean retro 70–80s typography, fast camera with speed ramp.
- **Product and promise:** a friendly host explains filmmaking in three steps: plan the movement, choose the shot, move the camera.
- **Format:** 1920x1080 @ 30fps, 10 s, Korean + English display words.
- **Platform and captions:** social/YouTube landscape; captions none (user banned subtitles).
- **Assets:** none supplied. Voice generated.
- **Subject:** illustrated man (live-action generation unavailable): white kopiah, white jubah, wooden folding chair. Same drawing in every scene (man.js).
- **Invented:** the illustration, the backdrops, the newspaper/poster/marquee/film-strip sets.

## Concept

- **Options:** (a) one continuous printed poster the camera flies across; (b) five printed "sets" swapped by hard cuts like flipping pages of a retro film magazine; (c) a single set where only typography changes.
- **Chosen:** (b) — it matches the user's per-2-second sets exactly and each cut gets its own camera verb.
- **Copy system:** statements — one Korean word + one English word per scene, never over face or hands.

## Look

- **Frame and palette:** cream #efe3c8, vermilion #e2432a, mustard #e0a526, navy #1d2c55, ink #1b1712; amber key #f2a33a left, teal #2fb3a8 right.
- **Type:** Black Han Sans (Hangul, blocky 70s gothic) + Alfa Slab One (English slab, retro cinema posters).
- **World:** flat ink-outlined figure with halftone shading, misregistered print offsets, grain.

## Sound

- **Mode:** voiceover + very light motion effects (no music, per brief).
- **Arc:** calm open → snappy stamp → counting → whip energy peak → slow settle.
